# threats to validity

- internal: order effects, fatigue, automation bias. mitigated by randomization and balanced latin-square (study 2), run-order randomization (study 1).
- construct: confidence calibration vs correctness; use brier/calibration error. quality held comparable for h2.
- statistical: multiple comparisons (holm), small sample sizes (report when test not appropriate), effect sizes with bootstrap 95% cis, mixed effects (task/participant random).
- external: single domain (software engineering); results may not generalize. transfer proposed via table.
- reliability: deterministic mocks where possible; seeds recorded; provider drift noted.
- ethical/operational: docker may be unavailable in some environments (sandbox refuses gracefully; no unsafe local mode). ethics review requirements vary by jurisdiction (see study2 materials with placeholders).
- third parties: harms often fall on non-users; limits bound impact but do not remove third-party risk.
